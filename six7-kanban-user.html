1. Getting started#

1.1 What Kanban is for#

Kanban keeps your work on boards. Each piece of work is a card, and each card moves across columns such as To do, Doing and Done as the work goes on. Everyone on the board sees where things stand, without asking. A board can also be a small project, with a goal, a target date and milestones.

Why Six7, and how its apps work, is in the Suite manual.

1.2 Opening the app#

Open the link you were given and bookmark it. Google signs you in: there's no separate password. Use the right Google account, as in Suite §5.

If the app says "Sign in with your work account" or "You don't have access to Six7 Kanban yet", see Suite §5.

1.3 Your role#

Solo

Kanban is yours alone. You're its Admin and the Board admin of every board, so everything in this manual is open to you. Sections about other people are marked Team: skip them.

Team

What you can do depends on two roles, shown at the top right under your name and on each board ("You: Editor"):

  • Your app role: Member (most people), Manager (sees every board) or Admin (also runs the app).
  • Your role on each board: Board admin (runs the board), Editor (works on cards) or Viewer (reads and comments).

Each task below says who can do it. The general model is in Suite §6.

2. A tour of the app#

The bar at the top has Boards, My cards, Calendar and Reports, a Search cards box, a refresh button, and your name, which opens Your settings. Admins also see Admin tools.

2.1 Boards, the home screen#

Every project you can see, at a glance. Each tile shows the board's status (On track, At risk, Off track or Finished), its goal and target date, the next milestone, this week's summary, the progress bar and the open and overdue cards.

Below the tiles, Open boards lists boards anyone in the company can view. Filter by Status and Member, or start a New board.

2.2 A board#

The board's header shows its status, goal, target date and next milestone, then This week, who can see the board, your role and its members. Filters narrow the cards by Member, Due, Label, Milestone or a word of the title.

Each card shows its number (#233), title, labels, due date (red when overdue), checklist progress, comments and the initials of the people on it.

A Triage column, drawn with a dashed edge, is where new requests land. A Not now column, last and folded, holds work the team has parked.

2.3 A card#

Click a card to open it beside the board, or full screen on a phone. Everything about the work is there: its column, the people on it, the due date, labels and milestone, the description, the checklist, the comments and the card's History.

2.4 My cards#

Everything assigned to you, across all boards, sorted by when it's due: Overdue, Today, This week, Later and No date. Then your parked cards under Not now, and the comments that mentioned you in the last 7 days. Done on a row moves the card to its board's Done column. Calendar at the top shows the same cards on a calendar.

2.5 Calendar#

Due dates, milestones and target dates on a Month or Week view, for one board or all. Use ‹, Today and › to move, and the Board, Member and Label filters to narrow it. Click an entry to open it. Dates are changed on the card, not on the calendar.

3. Everyday tasks#

3.1 Create a board#

Any Member can create a board, and becomes its Board admin.

  1. On Boards, click New board.
  2. Type its Name, and, if it's a project, its Goal and Target date.
  3. Choose Who can see it: Private (only its members) or Open (everyone in the company can view and comment). Team
  4. Choose the Columns to start with: Basic (To do, Doing, Done), Team workflow (Backlog, To do, In progress, Review, Done) or Project (Triage, To do, Doing, Done, Not now).
  5. Click Create board.

Team Add the board's members afterwards, in its Settings (§3.10).

3.2 Add cards#

  1. Click + Add card at the bottom of a column.
  2. Type the title and press Enter. The box stays open for the next card. Esc closes it.

You can fill in the card as you type, with these marks:

TypeTo setExample
@nameWho's on it: first name, full name or the start of their email@sofia
!dateThe due date: !today, !tomorrow, a weekday, a day of the month or a date!fri, !15, !5 nov
#labelA label of the board#delta
~milestoneA milestone of the board~training

Under the box, Kanban shows what it understood before you press Enter. A mark that matches nothing stays in the title, and the preview says so. To keep a mark as plain text, put \ before it: \@team.

3.3 Move a card#

Editors and Board admins can move cards.

  • With a mouse: drag the card to its new place.
  • On a touch screen: press and hold the card, then drag.
  • With Move to…: click Move to… on the card, choose the Column and Top or Bottom, then Move. This is the way on a phone, and from the keyboard (m).

The card moves at once. If someone archived it meanwhile, it leaves the board, with "Someone archived this card. It left the board."

Moving a card out of the first column (or Triage) marks it started; moving it into Done marks it finished. Reports and This week count from those moments.

3.4 Fill in a card#

Open the card. Editors and Board admins can change:

  • the title: click it and type;
  • Assignees: Change, then pick board members;
  • the Due date;
  • Labels: Change, then pick from the board's labels;
  • the Milestone: pick one of the board's milestones;
  • the Description: Edit, type, Save. Links in it can be clicked.

Team If someone else changed the same field while you were typing, the card shows who did it, and lets you choose Keep mine or Use theirs. Your other changes are saved.

3.5 Use a checklist#

Big cards move forward step by step:

  • type in Add an item, then Enter to add a step;
  • tick an item when it's done; the card shows the progress, such as Checklist 2/5;
  • click an item's text to rename it, use the arrows to reorder it, and × to remove it.

3.6 Comment and mention#

Anyone who can see a card can comment, Viewers included.

  1. Type in the box under Comments.
  2. Team Type @ to pick a board member. They get an email.
  3. Click Comment, or press Ctrl+Enter.

You can edit or remove your own comment for 15 minutes. Board admins can remove any comment.

3.7 Triage and Not now#

  • Triage is where new requests arrive, for example from an import. Look at it often: move each card on to a column, or park it.
  • Not now parks work the team has decided not to do yet. Parked cards don't count as open, don't show as due in My cards, and stay out of the reports' open counts. Click Fold or Show on the column to hide or show its cards.

3.8 Archive and restore a card#

Nothing in Kanban is ever deleted.

  • Archive: open the card and click Archive. It leaves the board, My cards and search.
  • Restore: on the board, click Archived cards, then Restore beside the card.

3.9 Run the board as a project#

Board admins set this in the board's Settings, under Project:

  • the Goal, one line;
  • the Target date;
  • the Status: On track, At risk, Off track or Finished, with The status in a line to say why.

Milestones, further down, are the dates that matter on the way: give each a name and a date, and attach cards to it (§3.4). Tick Reached when it's done. The board and the home screen show the next milestone and how many of its cards are done.

This week on the board sums up the week so far: cards finished, started and created, cards stuck in a column for 7 days or more, overdue cards, the next milestone and the target date, with the status line. Team Managers and Board admins who get the report email read it every Monday.

3.10 Set up columns, labels and members#

Board admins, in the board's Settings. Columns, labels and milestones save as you change them:

  • Columns: Add column, rename, reorder with the arrows, and set each column's kind: Done, Normal, Triage or Not now. A board has at most one Triage and one Not now column. Archive a column you no longer need: if it still has cards, move them to another column or archive them with it. A Triage or Not now column can be archived only when it's empty.
  • Labels: Add label, with a name and one of seven colours, up to 20 per board. Removing a label takes it off its cards.
  • Team Members: add people from your company by name or email, and give each a role: Board admin, Editor or Viewer. A board keeps at least one Board admin. Removing someone doesn't take them off their cards: the Board admins' daily digest lists those cards, so you can reassign them.
  • Team Who can see it: Private or Open.

Click Save for the name, description, project, visibility and members.

3.11 Archive a board#

When a project is over, a Board admin can set its status to Finished and keep it readable, or archive it: Settings › Archive board. Its members no longer see it. Team A Manager or an Admin can restore it from Archived boards.

4. Working with others Team#

  • Live board. While a board is open, other people's moves, edits and comments appear within about 10 seconds while you're using it, or a minute after a few idle minutes; yours appear at once. "All changes saved" in the header confirms your own. The board stops checking after 15 minutes without use, and starts again when you click.
  • Two people, one card. The last move wins, and both screens show where the card ended up. If you both edit the same field, you choose Keep mine or Use theirs (§3.4). Ticking different checklist items never clashes.
  • Mentions and assignments email the person, unless they chose to get them in their digest (§8).
  • Open boards can be read and commented on by everyone in the company. Only members edit them.
  • Who sees a private board: its members, plus Managers and Admins.
  • Copy link on a board or a card copies its address, to paste in an email or chat. Only people who can see it can open it.

5. Finding things#

  • Search cards, at the top: type a word of a title, or a card's number (87 or #87). It searches every board you can see. At the bottom of the results, Search older cards also looks through cards finished long ago.
  • The palette: press / (or Ctrl+K) anywhere to find a board, a card or an action, then Enter.
  • Board filters: Member, Due, Label, Milestone, or Filter by title or #number.
  • Archived cards on a board, and Team Archived boards on the home screen for Managers and Admins.

6. Reports#

Reports shows six reports, each with a chart, a table and a sentence that sums it up. Choose the Board, Member and Weeks back at the top.

ReportAnswers
ThroughputHow many cards were finished each week?
Cycle timeHow long does a card take from started to done?
Cumulative flowHow many cards sat in each column each week? (one board)
Due datesHow many cards finished on time, and how many are overdue?
Workload by memberWho has how many open, in-progress and overdue cards?
What movedWhat was finished, started, parked and stuck each week? (one board)

Each report has Export to Sheets, Generate report (Doc) and Download PDF. At the top, Generate full report (Doc) and Download full PDF make one document with all six. Copy link shares the report with the same choices.

The scope line says what's counted: Team "Your boards" for a Member, "All boards" for a Manager.

7. For managers Team#

As a Manager:

  • the home screen shows every board, private ones included, sorted with the projects Off track and At risk first;
  • reports cover every board;
  • the boards overview email arrives every Monday by default: cards finished, cycle time, open and overdue cards, each board's What moved, and what needs attention (overdue cards by person, cards in progress for more than 14 days, boards with no activity, projects at risk and missed dates). An Admin can make it daily;
  • you can act as Board admin on any board, for example to name a new Board admin when one leaves;
  • you can restore archived boards.

8. Email and notifications#

Kanban emails you when:

  • Team someone assigns you to a card or mentions you in a comment. At most one email per card every 10 minutes; the rest wait for your digest;
  • you have cards overdue or due today (and, on Mondays, due this week): the daily digest, around 07:00, only on days there's something to say.

To change them, click your name at the top right, Your settings:

  • Notifications: Email me at once, or Keep them for my daily digest;
  • tick or untick the daily digest;
  • Theme: System, Light or Dark, for this browser.

10. Using it on a phone#

Kanban works in your phone's browser; there's no app to install.

  • A board shows one column at a time: swipe sideways for the next.
  • Move cards with Move to… (§3.3).
  • A card opens full screen.
  • The calendar shows a week, one day per row.
  • Open links from Kanban emails in your browser, not inside the Gmail app.

11. When something goes wrong#

What you seeWhat it means
"Changed by …" on a card you're editing TeamSomeone else saved the same field. Choose Keep mine or Use theirs
"Someone archived this card. It left the board."It was archived while you moved it. Find it in Archived cards
"This item was removed" TeamSomeone removed that checklist item first
"The app is busy, please try again" or "Google is busy right now"Many changes at once. Try again in a moment
Other people's changes don't show TeamThe board stopped checking after a while without use. Click anywhere, or use the refresh button at the top
"That board or card doesn't exist, or you can't see it."It's archived, or you're not on that board
"Six7 Kanban is parked"Kanban is stopped for now, and nothing is lost. Solo Bring it back from the Sheet: Six7 › Danger zone › Resume. Team Ask your Kanban Admin when it will be back

Who to ask: Solo Six7 (Suite §10). Team Your Kanban Admin first.
